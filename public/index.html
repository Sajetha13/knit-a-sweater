How To Play

1. Pick Your Fit

Easy: 4-key back-and-forth row weave.
Medium: 6-key cardigan + break words.
Hard: 4-key alternating cable combos.
3rd of Dec: type the lyrics straight up.

2. Stay in Rhythm

Hit the glowing keys before the timer hits 00:00. Every stitch fills the sweater with yarn from bottom to top.

3. Zero Red Dots Allowed

Miss a key and a red dot drops onto the wool. You can keep going—but if there's even one red dot left at the end, the whole sweater unravels. Now go knit.
